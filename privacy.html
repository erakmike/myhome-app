Privacy

Updated September 30, 2026.

myHome doesn't collect, sell or share your information. The developer receives no data from the app. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your home

myHome talks directly to your own Home Assistant hub. Your devices, cameras, rooms and settings stay between the app and your hub. Your sign-in is stored in your device's secure Keychain.

Services it uses only when you do

  • Weather: the U.S. National Weather Service (weather.gov) receives the map location of the ZIP code you enter, to return a forecast.
  • Asking questions (optional): if you use Ask, your request is sent to Anthropic's Claude to be understood, using a key you provide.
  • Spoken replies (optional): if you turn on the ElevenLabs voice, the reply text is sent to ElevenLabs to be read aloud, using a key you provide.
  • Talking to the app: Apple's speech recognition turns what you say into text, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Calendar, Reminders and photos: read on your device only, if you allow it, and never sent anywhere.

Children

myHome isn't directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
