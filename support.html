Help

myHome works with a Home Assistant hub on your home network.

Getting started

  • Open myHome. It starts in a practice house, so you can look around without a hub.
  • To connect your home, go to Settings and sign in with your Home Assistant address, username and password.
  • Away from home, connect to your home network first (for example with your router's VPN).
